Phazite Sounds
==============

85 free sounds for Phazite: drums, 808s, loops, effects and foley sounds, as 44.1 kHz FLAC files. Phazite's
browser lists them under "Phazite Sounds", and together with your own sounds under "Sounds by Type". They work
in any other program that reads FLAC, too.


Use them in anything
--------------------

Every sound here is dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal (LICENSE.txt). You can use them in
any song, sell what you make with them, and change them, without asking or giving credit.


Where they come from
--------------------

Sample libraries that dedicate their sounds to CC0:
  - the Versilian Community Sample Library and Virtuosity Drums, by Versilian Studios;
  - Big Rusty Drums, by Karoryfer Samples;
  - body_percussion, from sfzinstruments;
  - FreePats Synthesizer Percussion, by Roberto, made with the Yoshimi and Geonkick synthesizers;
  - Sonic Pi's samples, from Freesound users soneproject, Halgrimm, EcoDTR and maqsim;
  - a 108 BPM breakbeat that EatMe recorded and put in the public domain;
  - RPG Audio and Impact Sounds, by Kenney.
Credit isn't required, but they deserve it.

Phazite's own: kicks, 808s, analog-style drums and effects that Phazite's sound tool synthesizes, and loops it
plays from these sounds, to patterns written for Phazite. Their makers dedicate them to the public domain under
CC0 1.0 as well.

sources.json says how each sound was made. For a sound taken from a library, it links to the original file at
the version it was taken from, and says what shows that file is CC0.

How the sounds are made, and how to suggest new ones: https://github.com/august-byrne/phazite-sounds
